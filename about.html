Our Mission & Vision

RISE is a student-run organization in Atlanta. We teach STEM through hands-on projects—from building bridges to testing gliders—and share what we learn through camps, workshops, and DIY kits.

Mission

To make STEM education more inclusive, accessible, and empowering for every student.

Vision

We envision a world where all young people, regardless of background, can rise to their full potential through equitable access to science, technology, engineering, and math education.

Past Partners

A look at the community partnerships that have helped us bring hands-on STEM learning to students.

Agape Youth & Family Center

At Agape, we led a four-day summer camp introducing middle school students to civil, mechanical, chemical, and aerospace engineering. Our work together also included Python classes, bringing programming into the mix alongside hands-on engineering projects.

Visit Agape

LaAmistad

Through our partnership with LaAmistad, we introduced elementary school students to STEM through hands-on workshops. Ice cream experiments, rocket projects, and candy-tower challenges gave students different ways to explore science and engineering.

Fulton County Public Libraries

Our workshops at Fulton County libraries gave students the chance to put their ideas to the test. From constructing popsicle-stick bridges to launching rockets, students explored engineering by building and experimenting.
